Apps

RedScreen

A free Windows tray app that makes your screen warm, red and dim in the evening. Make your own modes and schedule them by the clock. It never connects to the internet.

Open source (MIT) · Windows 10 and 11

Bindy

A helper on your Windows screen. Press a hotkey, ask a question, and a small paperclip points at what to click. It uses your own Claude account.

Open source (MIT) · Windows 10 and 11
