Publish to TikTok

A small tool for creators and publishers. Sign in with TikTok, choose how your video is shared, and publish it to your own account. Nothing is posted until you choose a visibility and confirm.

1. Sign in

The tool asks for two permissions: user.info.basic (to show which account you are posting to) and video.publish (to publish the video you choose to that account).
